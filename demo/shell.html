Tidewrack

Sorts what washes up. Filter by tag, category or year, search, sort, cast a line, read in a pop-up, and switch layouts. This page runs the real module template, stylesheet and script (version {{VERSION}}) with sample articles.

Click a card to read it in the pop-up; ← → step through the articles that match your filters, and the address bar keeps your place, so the link opens the same article. The sidebar sticks beside the cards from 992px wide; narrower, it goes back on top. Three sample articles have no image, so they show the no-image art.
